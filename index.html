Chess Shorts Bot

Chess Shorts Bot is a personal automation tool run by Eren Berk Gülmez. It finds brilliant sacrifices in famous and recent chess games, renders a short vertical video for each one, and uploads it to Eren's own YouTube channel with the YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to other users. It only signs in to, and uploads to, the channel owner's own YouTube account.
