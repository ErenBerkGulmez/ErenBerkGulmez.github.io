Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Chess Shorts Bot ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Eren Berk Gülmez to publish chess videos to his own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

The app signs in with Google OAuth to the channel owner's own Google account only, using these scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the videos it creates.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to post one comment (the puzzle solution) under each uploaded video.

It does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Google account data, and it does not access data of any other user.

What is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's private server so the app can upload on schedule. No personal data of viewers or other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
